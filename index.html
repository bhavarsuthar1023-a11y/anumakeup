About Me

Welcome to Makeover by anusoni, where beauty meets artistry. Based in Sirohi, Rajasthan, we are dedicated to transforming your vision into a stunning reality. With years of expertise in the beauty industry, our mission is to enhance your natural features while ensuring you feel confident and empowered on your most special days.

We specialize in a wide array of services including luxury bridal makeovers, high-definition celebrity looks, and elegant event styling. Our approach combines state-of-the-art techniques with premium products to deliver a flawless, long-lasting finish that looks spectacular both in person and through the lens.

At Makeover by anusoni, every stroke is intentional, and every look is personalized. Whether it’s a grand wedding or an intimate celebration, we provide a premium, relaxing experience
